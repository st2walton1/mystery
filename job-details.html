Position

Mystery Shopper

Part-time / Flexible

Mystery Shoppers visit assigned shops, supermarkets, restaurants, or other businesses to evaluate customer service and the customer experience. They then provide honest and accurate feedback through a simple evaluation report.

Employment typePart-time / Flexible
LocationRemote coordination; assigned visits may be in person
ExperiencePrevious experience not compulsory

Job responsibilities

Tasks may vary by assignment. Follow the specific instructions provided for each approved assignment.

  • 01
    Visit assigned businesses

    Visit shops, supermarkets, restaurants, or other businesses when an assignment requires an in-person visit.

  • 02
    Evaluate customer service

    Observe staff attitude, service quality, product presentation, and the overall customer experience.

  • 03
    Complete evaluation reports

    Record the experience using the reporting format and instructions provided.

  • 04
    Provide accurate feedback

    Submit honest, clear, and factual observations within the agreed deadline.

Requirements

  • ✓ Be observant and reliable.
  • ✓ Have good communication and reporting skills.
  • ✓ Be able to follow instructions and meet deadlines.
  • ✓ Previous mystery shopping experience is an advantage but is not compulsory.

What the opportunity offers

  • ✓ Flexible working hours, depending on available assignments.
  • ✓ An opportunity to gain valuable work experience.
  • ✓ Payment for completed and approved assignments according to agreed terms.

Payment amounts, assignment availability, reimbursement arrangements, and payment timing should be confirmed in writing before accepting an assignment. No specific income or number of assignments is guaranteed on this website.

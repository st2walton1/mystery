Before accepting an assignment

Will every assignment be remote?

Not necessarily. The role may involve visiting assigned shops, supermarkets, restaurants, or other businesses. Confirm the location and visit requirements for each assignment.

Is previous experience required?

No. Previous experience is an advantage, but the opportunity information says it is not compulsory.

Do applicants need to pay a fee?

No recruitment fee should be required to secure this position. Be cautious if anyone asks for payment to obtain the job.
